# TwistRide Support

TwistRide is a turn-by-turn motorcycle navigation app for US riders. This page covers common questions and how to get help.

## Contact

Open an issue in the support repository:

https://github.com/lookfuyao/twistride-support/issues

## Getting started

1. Open TwistRide and allow location access while using the app.
2. Tap **Search destination** and choose a destination, or long-press the map to drop a pin.
3. Review the route and tap **Start navigation**.
4. Tap **End** and confirm to finish.

## GPX import

- Open the map screen and tap the document button in the top bar.
- Choose a GPX 1.0/1.1 file from Files or another app.
- TwistRide snaps the track to roads when the routing service is available. If snapping fails, the original track is used and a notice is shown.

## Offline maps

- Open **Settings → Offline maps**.
- Download a region. Downloads are Wi-Fi only by default; you can turn that off on the same screen.
- The app checks available storage before downloading. If storage is low, delete an old pack or free space and retry.

## Subscriptions

- Pro unlocks Slightly/Very Curvy routing, loop rides, offline map packs, and corner warnings.
- Open **Settings → Upgrade to Pro** to subscribe.
- Use **Restore purchases** on the same screen if you already subscribed.
- Manage or cancel in your Apple ID subscription settings.

## Corner warnings

- Corner warnings are a Pro feature and are enabled by default for Pro.
- Warnings are advisory only. Always follow posted speed limits, road signs, and conditions.
- Warnings require location updates; they will not appear if the app is not receiving movement.

## Privacy

TwistRide requires no account and does not upload your ride history. See the [privacy policy](privacy.html) for details.

## Safety

Do not operate your phone while riding. Mount your phone securely. Navigation and warnings are advisory and do not replace traffic laws or road signs.
