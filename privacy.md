# TwistRide Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-11-05
**Last updated:** 2026-11-05

TwistRide is a navigation app for motorcycle riders. This policy explains what information the app processes and how it is handled.

## Summary

- No account is required.
- Your routes, GPX files, ride history, and settings are stored on your device.
- We do not upload your ride history.
- Routing requests send anonymous origin/destination coordinates to a routing service so a route can be calculated.
- We do not sell personal information, show ads, or use tracking across apps or websites.

## Information processed by the app

### Location

TwistRide uses your device's location to show your position, calculate routes, provide turn-by-turn guidance, and record rides.

When you request a route, the app sends the origin and destination coordinates, and any waypoints, to a routing service. The request does not include your name, account, advertising identifier, or other persistent identifier.

Location history and ride tracks are stored locally on your device unless you explicitly export or share them.

### Map tiles

The app requests map tiles from a map tile service. As with any network request, the service may receive your IP address and standard request metadata. We do not use this to identify you.

### Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase transaction.

### Local data

Routes, favorites, GPX files, ride recordings, and app settings are stored on your device. You can delete rides and offline map packs from the app.

### Diagnostics and analytics

The current version does not include third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. If crash reporting or anonymous analytics is added in a future version, this policy will be updated before release.

## Service providers

Routing may be provided by Stadia Maps or a self-hosted Valhalla instance. Map tiles may be provided by OpenFreeMap or a self-hosted tile service. These providers process the technical request needed to return a route or tile.

Map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors and is available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0.

## Data retention

Routing requests are designed not to include persistent identifiers. Server-side request logs are intended to be deleted within 24 hours. Locally stored rides and GPX files remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app.

## Your choices

- You can deny or revoke location access in iOS Settings. Navigation and ride recording will not work without location access.
- You can delete rides and offline map packs in the app.
- You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.

## Children

TwistRide is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## California residents

We do not sell personal information. If you have questions about your California privacy rights, contact us using the details below.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top will be updated when we do.
